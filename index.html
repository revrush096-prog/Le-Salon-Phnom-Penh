THE LE SALON EXPERIENCE

Where Beauty Becomes an Experience

Situated in the heart of Phnom Penh on Preah Ang Non, Le Salon was conceived as an intentional sanctuary where personal style, professional beauty services, relaxation, and meticulous attention to detail converge.

Drawing inspiration from classic Parisian ateliers, our space blends understated French minimalism with warm Cambodian hospitality. Every appointment is crafted as a personalized ritual—designed to celebrate your individuality while providing a calm escape from the bustling city outside.
